# LockedIn High Fidelity UI Generation Prompts

## Global Design Direction (Apply to Every Screen)

Design a premium mobile application called **LockedIn**, a gamified daily mission and accountability platform.

The app should feel like a combination of:

* Apple Fitness
* Duolingo progression
* premium fintech applications
* modern AI interfaces
* RPG character progression systems

Do not design it like a traditional productivity app or checklist.

Core feeling:

"Become the person you promised yourself you would become."

Design principles:

* Mobile first iOS quality
* Dark mode first
* Premium consumer SaaS aesthetic
* Minimal typing
* Highly interactive cards
* Smooth animations
* Strong hierarchy
* Emotional engagement
* Gamified progression
* Professional enough for Apple App Store featuring

Visual style:

* Midnight black backgrounds
* Glassmorphism cards
* Soft gradients
* Premium typography
* Subtle glow effects
* Elegant animations
* Clean spacing
* Rounded corners
* High-end startup design

Typography:

SF Pro Display inspired.

Components:

* Mission cards
* XP progress rings
* Achievement badges
* Avatar progression
* AI assistant elements
* Interactive buttons
* Reward animations
