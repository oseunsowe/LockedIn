Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Applies to the LockedIn mobile app and lockedinmission.app

LockedIn helps you turn commitments into missions, prove you did them, and track your progress. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. We keep it plain and specific to what the app actually does.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (or your Google/Apple sign-in identifier), and the display name you choose.
  • Profile and progress: your chosen identity class and goals, level, XP, streaks, achievements, time zone and (optionally) a profile picture you upload.
  • Missions: the titles, deadlines, difficulty and proof requirements of missions you create, and their outcomes.
  • Proof you submit: photos, screenshots or files you attach as proof, and the result of the AI check (verdict, confidence, and a short explanation).
  • Screenshots you scan (optional): if you use Screenshot Intelligence, the screenshots you select are analysed to find goals. They are processed in memory and discarded immediately; they are not stored.
  • Focus sessions: the start time and length of Focus Mode sessions you run.
  • Subscription status: your plan (free or paid) and its dates. Payments are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your card details.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we do not show ads.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, store your missions and progress, and sync them across sessions.
  • To verify proof and scan screenshots using AI, when you ask us to.
  • To enforce fair-use limits and keep the service secure.
  • To send reminders you turn on. Reminders are scheduled on your device; they are not sent from our servers.
  • To respond to support requests.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • Anthropic processes the proof images and screenshots you submit so that its AI model can produce a verification result or extract goals. We send only what is needed for that request.
  • Google (and Apple on iOS) process sign-in if you choose those options, and Google Play processes any purchases.

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

Where your data is kept

Proof files are stored in a private storage area that only your account can access; profile pictures are stored in a public image folder so they can be displayed, and are linked only to your account. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each table is restricted to the account that owns the data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, missions, proofs, verification results, XP history, achievements, focus sessions, subscription record and stored files (including your profile picture) are permanently deleted. Routine backups may retain copies for a short period before they roll off.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Delete Account, or follow lockedinmission.app/delete-account.html. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Access or correct your data: most of it is visible in the app; you can edit your name and picture in Profile. Email us for anything else.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Profile → Notifications or in your phone settings.
  • Photos and camera: LockedIn only accesses images you choose, or that you capture when you tap the camera option.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (such as access, portability, objection or complaint to a data-protection authority). Email us and we will help.

Children

LockedIn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Our website

lockedinmission.app records a small number of first-party events (for example, page views and whether a sign-up succeeded) together with campaign tags from the link you arrived on. It sets no advertising cookies, uses no third-party trackers, and respects your browser's Do Not Track setting. If you join the early-access list we store your email address (and your platform choice) to notify you at launch; every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Changes

If we make material changes we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
