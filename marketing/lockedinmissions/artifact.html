Questions

What is LockedIn, exactly?

A mission-based accountability app. You turn a real goal into a mission with a deadline and a proof requirement, submit evidence when you're done, and an AI verifies it — rather than trusting an honor-system checkbox.

When does it launch?

We're finishing Google Play developer account setup now. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email the moment it's live — nothing before that.

Is it free?

Yes, with a free daily allowance of AI verifications and screenshot scans that's generous enough to run the app for real. Higher-volume tiers are planned for anyone who wants more per day.

What actually happens to my photos?

Proof photos are stored privately and used only to verify that specific mission. Screenshots submitted to Screenshot Intelligence are analyzed and discarded immediately — there's no storage bucket for them at all. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

iOS too?

Yes — LockedIn ships on both iOS and Android.
