LockedIn (lockedinmission.app)

You can permanently delete your LockedIn account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open LockedIn and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't access the app?

Email hello@lockedinmission.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my LockedIn account". We will delete the account and confirm by email, normally within 7 days.

Deletion is permanent. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, display name, goals and profile picture
  • All missions, proof files and verification results
  • XP history, level, streaks and achievements
  • Focus sessions and your subscription record
  • Your sign-in (email or linked Google/Apple identity)

What may remain

Encrypted backups may hold copies for a short period before they are overwritten. If you bought a subscription, your purchase record stays with Google Play; cancel the subscription in Google Play to stop future charges. Early-access list emails can be removed with the unsubscribe link in any email.
